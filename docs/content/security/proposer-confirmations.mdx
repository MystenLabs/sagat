---
title: Proposer confirmations
description: How the app checks who proposed a proposal and whether the pod’s signers confirmed its external proposers.
---

The API records who proposed each proposal and which signer added each external proposer. The app doesn’t rely on that record alone: it checks the wallet signatures stored with it. The service holds none of the signers’ or proposers’ keys, so it can’t make those signatures itself.

## Confirmed proposals

A proposal is confirmed when it comes from:

- a signer who signed it, when proposing it or, if that signature isn’t on record, by approving it
- an external proposer who signed it when proposing it, and who is [confirmed](#confirmed-proposers)

If one of these signatures doesn’t check out, the app refuses to sign. A proposal that is otherwise not confirmed, for example one proposed before signatures were kept, gets a warning.

## If you see a warning or a refusal

- **Proposer not confirmed:** nothing on record shows it came from a signer or a confirmed external proposer. Check the transaction in the app’s preview and your wallet’s before you sign.
- **The proposer’s confirmation doesn’t check out:** the app won’t sign the proposal. If it came from an external proposer you don’t recognize, remove them on the pod’s **Members** page. Removing them also cancels their pending proposals.

## Confirmed proposers

When a signer adds an external proposer, their wallet signs a message that names the proposer, the pod and an expiry, and the service keeps that signature. A proposer is confirmed when the message that last added them is signed by a key in the pod’s current signer set. The app counts those keys only when they, their weights and the threshold make up the pod’s address.

- **Confirmed:** a current signer’s signed message adding them is on record and checks out.
- **Unverified:** no signature the app can check is on record, for example for a proposer added before signatures were kept. Any signer can re-confirm the proposer by adding them again.
- **Doesn’t check out:** the signature on record doesn’t match, or wasn’t made by a current signer. The app refuses to sign that proposer’s proposals. Remove the proposer if you don’t recognize it.

## What a confirmation doesn’t prove

- Confirmations reach the app through the API. One shows that a current signer added the proposer, not that the API returned every addition and removal.
- Only keys in the current signer set count. A key that leaves the signer set no longer confirms anyone.
- One signer is enough to add or re-confirm a proposer. It doesn’t take the threshold.
- A confirmation says nothing about a proposal’s transaction. [Check what you sign](/security/trust-model#check-what-you-sign) in the app’s preview and your wallet’s.

Confirmations are a record kept by the service, not part of Sui: when a transaction executes, Sui checks only the signers’ combined signature. Integrators can run the same checks with the SDK’s verify functions, such as `verifyProposer` and `verifyProposalProposer`, after checking that the signers they pass make up the pod’s address.
