---
title: Multisig accounts on Sui
description: Create a multisig account with other signers, propose transactions, collect signatures and execute them on Sui.
---

Sui Pods is an app, an API and a TypeScript SDK for running multisig accounts on Sui. You and your co-signers share one account address, propose transactions from it, and execute each one once enough of you have signed.

Multisig is native to Sui, and the service adds nothing of its own on chain: your account is a standard Sui multisig address. Whatever you approve is signed in your own wallet.
