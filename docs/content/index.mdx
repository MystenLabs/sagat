---
title: Multisig accounts on Sui
description: Create a pod, a multisig account, with other signers, propose transactions, collect signatures and execute them on Sui.
---

Sui Pods is an app, an API and a TypeScript SDK for running multisig accounts on Sui. You and your co-signers share one multisig account, called a pod, propose transactions from its address, and execute each one once enough of you have signed.

Multisig is native to Sui, and the service adds nothing of its own on chain: your pod is a standard Sui multisig address. Whatever you approve is signed in your own wallet.
