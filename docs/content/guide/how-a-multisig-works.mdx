## What a pod is

A pod is a multisig account: a Sui address that several people control together. Instead of one private key, it has a list of [signers](/glossary#signer), a [weight](/glossary#weight) for each, and a [threshold](/glossary#threshold). A transaction from the pod goes through only when the signers who signed it have enough weight between them to meet the threshold.

Multisig is [built into Sui](https://docs.sui.io/develop/transactions/transaction-auth/multisig). The pod’s address is derived from the signers’ public keys, their weights and the threshold, so there is no smart contract to deploy. The app shows a live preview of the address while you create the pod.

The app and the API only coordinate: they store proposals and signatures and show who has signed. The pod is a standard Sui multisig address, so anyone who knows its public keys, weights and threshold can derive it and use it with [other Sui tooling](https://sdk.mystenlabs.com/sui/cryptography/signers/multisig), without this service. See the [trust model](/security/trust-model#multisig-is-native-to-sui).

## Signers and public keys

Each signer is one public key, usually the key behind your wallet. When you create a pod, each signer’s field takes either a public key or an address (**Paste a public key, or search by address**). Paste an address and the app looks for its public key in the service’s records and on chain at the same time, and fills in the key as soon as either has it. That gives three ways to add someone, easiest first:

1. **They sign in to the app once.** They choose **Connect wallet**, then **Sign in**. Signing in registers the public key of the account they signed in with, so from then on their address is enough.
2. **Their address has sent a transaction.** The app reads the public key from the signature of a transaction the address has sent on mainnet, testnet or devnet.
3. **You paste their public key.** If the app shows “No public key found for this address”, ask the signer for their public key and paste it instead of the address.

The app accepts Ed25519, Secp256k1 and Secp256r1 keys.

A pod has between 2 and 10 signers. When you create one, any of your own keys in the list accept right away. Every other signer gets an [invitation](/glossary#invitation) to accept or reject, by signing a message with their wallet. The pod shows up in your list once you have accepted, and the app marks signers who have not answered yet as **Invited**.

## Weights and the threshold

Each signer has a weight, a whole number from 1 to 255. The threshold is a whole number from 1 up to the total weight of all signers. A proposal is ready to execute when the weights of the signers who signed it add up to the threshold or more.

### A worked example

Three signers, threshold 3:

| Signer | Weight |
| ------ | ------ |
| Alice  | 2      |
| Bob    | 1      |
| Carol  | 1      |

| Who signed      | Total weight | Ready to execute |
| --------------- | ------------ | ---------------- |
| Alice           | 2            | No               |
| Bob and Carol   | 2            | No               |
| Alice and Bob   | 3            | Yes              |
| Alice and Carol | 3            | Yes              |
| All three       | 4            | Yes              |

Alice cannot act alone, and Bob and Carol cannot act without her.

## Why the address depends on the setup

The address is a hash of the threshold and of every signer’s public key and weight, in the order you list them. Change any of these and you get a different address. That is why the address preview changes as you edit the form, and why the app refuses a second pod with exactly the same signers, weights and threshold.

An existing pod’s signers, weights and threshold cannot be edited. A different setup is a different pod, with its own address and its own balances.

## Pods and networks

A pod and its address are the same on every Sui network. Proposals and balances are not: each proposal belongs to the [network](/glossary#network) it was created on, and the app shows the proposals and balances of the network you have selected.
